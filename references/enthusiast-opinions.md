# Enthusiast Opinions

What car enthusiasts believe, in their own "we" voice. These are opinions. They give the skill its point of view and a first guess at where a car sits.

## How to use them

- **Label it.** Offer a take as the enthusiast view: "The enthusiast take is…". The skill's own voice stays neutral.
- **Give the counter with it.** Every take below has one. A take without its counter is a slogan.
- **Never let a take decide a verdict.** The verdict comes from this car's facts: its price, its history, its failure modes, each one looked up. A take can point to what to check.
- **Mind the scope.** A take about belt CVTs says nothing about a hybrid. Outside its scope, a take is wrong.
- **Use them sparingly.** One take that fits the question beats three that don't.

Terms. A CVT (continuously variable transmission) uses a belt and pulleys instead of fixed gears. A plug-in hybrid has a gas engine plus a battery that charges from the wall. Naturally aspirated means no turbocharger.

## Rules of thumb

- **Skip the first year of a redesign.** Let someone else find the bugs. *Counter:* a carryover engine and transmission in a new body carry less risk.
- **The last year of a generation is the sweet spot.** Bugs fixed, discounts on, parts everywhere. *Counter:* older tech and safety equipment, and it looks dated sooner.
- **Lease the complicated, buy the simple.** We don't own a complex luxury car out of warranty unless we wrench or budget for it. *Counter:* a well-kept one with records and a good independent shop can be a bargain.
- **Buy the owner, not the car.** Service records beat the badge. A maintained BMW outlasts a neglected Camry. *Counter:* records don't fix a design flaw.
- **Find the independent specialist before the car.** Then have that shop inspect it. This matters most for a European car out of warranty. *Counter:* warranty and recall work still goes to the dealer.
- **"Lifetime" fluid is a lie.** We change transmission and differential fluid whatever the manual says. "Lifetime" tends to mean the warranty's lifetime. *Counter:* carmakers disagree, and a wrong fluid or a careless service can cause the failure.
- **Nothing is more expensive than a cheap German car.** The cheapest one on the lot is deferred maintenance changing hands. *Counter:* the right engine with records breaks the rule.
- **Stock beats modified.** Never pay extra for someone else's modifications. *Counter:* quality parts with receipts from a careful owner can be fine. Price it as stock.

## Appliance versus fun

- **Every car sits on one line.** Reliable appliance at one end, premium with a maintenance tax at the other. Know where the car sits before arguing about it. *Counter:* a few cars cheat the line, the Miata and the simple Mazdas among them.
- **Appliance brands hide fun models.** GR Corolla, GR86, Civic Si, Civic Type R and Elantra N are the real thing. *Counter:* dealer markups and insurance can erase the value case.
- **Miata is always the answer.** Cheap, light, reliable and fun at legal speeds. *Counter:* two seats and a small trunk, so it answers a narrow question.
- **Slow car fast beats fast car slow.** Using all of a modest car is more fun than using a third of a quick one. *Counter:* power is fun too, and highway passing matters.
- **A wagon or hot hatch beats the crossover.** Same job, less weight, better to drive. *Counter:* buyers want the seat height, and the US wagon market is nearly gone.
- **Save the manuals.** A manual makes a slow car fun and a fun car better. *Counter:* modern automatics are quicker, and a manual narrows the resale market on an ordinary car.
- **EVs are fast appliances.** Quick in a straight line and silent everywhere else. *Counter:* a few were built to answer this, the Ioniq 5 N and the Taycan among them.

## Drivetrain instincts

- **No belt CVTs.** They drone, and the bad ones die early. *Counter:* Toyota's hybrid "eCVT" shares only the name. It's a gearset with two electric motors and no belt. Honda's CVTs are well regarded too.
- **The ZF 8-speed is never the worry.** If a car has it, look elsewhere for problems. *Counter:* it still wants its fluid changed.
- **Naturally aspirated beats turbo for the long haul.** Fewer parts, less heat, fewer ways to die. *Counter:* some turbo engines are trusted, and some of the worst recent failures have no turbo.
- **Some engines are bought on name.** Toyota's 2JZ, Honda's K-series, GM's LS, the Lexus 5.0 V8, BMW's N52. *Counter:* the name adds a price premium, and a neglected one is still neglected.
- **A regular hybrid is the safe drivetrain.** *Counter:* that reputation leans on Toyota, Lexus and Honda. A weaker maker's hybrid doesn't inherit it.
- **A plug-in hybrid is two drivetrains to break.** *Counter:* the brand matters more than the plug.
- **Toyota's hybrid system is the most proven thing on sale.** Decades of taxi duty settled it. *Counter:* it's also the most appliance-like drivetrain there is.

## Brands, in one line

- **Toyota and Honda:** reliable appliances. *Counter:* the fun models are real, and both have shipped bad engines.
- **Mazda:** what we tell normal people to buy. It drives well and holds up. *Counter:* that's the simple ones. The newer, pricier models need time.
- **German cars:** worth the complexity if newer or well kept. *Counter:* judge by the engine and the service records, never by the year alone.
- **Lexus:** luxury without the maintenance tax. *Counter:* we also call most of them boring.
- **Porsche:** the German exception, built better than its neighbors. *Counter:* parts and labor stay expensive even when little breaks.
- **Jeep:** buy the feeling, lease the car. Nothing else is a Wrangler. *Counter:* that's the whole argument for owning one.
- **Range Rover:** the best place to sit and the worst thing to own at year six. *Counter:* "always broken" is stronger than the data.
- **Tesla:** the drivetrain and the charging network are the product. Build quality and service are the price. *Counter:* the build quality has improved.

## The used market

- **The Toyota tax is real.** You pay extra for the reputation, sometimes too much. *Counter:* high resale pays some of it back. Run the total cost before calling it overpriced.
- **Depreciated luxury is the cheapest way in and the dearest way to stay.** A five-year-old flagship costs Camry money to buy and flagship money to fix. *Counter:* that depreciation is the opportunity, with records and an inspection.

## Reputations that lag reality

- **BMW ranks better than its reputation.** "BMWs always break" is out of date for the newer engines. *Counter:* a brand score covers new cars under warranty. Year ten is a different question.
- **"Toyota and Honda are coasting" is half right.** Each has shipped a bad engine lately. *Counter:* both still sit near the top of the reliability surveys.
- **Korean cars moved both ways at once.** They build real enthusiast cars now, and they still carry engine and theft baggage. *Counter:* both are true, so judge by model and year.
- **The Tesla panel-gap jokes are getting old.** *Counter:* service access still draws complaints.
